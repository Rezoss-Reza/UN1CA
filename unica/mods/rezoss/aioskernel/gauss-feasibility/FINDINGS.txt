Samsung Gauss V73 feasibility, 2026-10-14

The exported Samsung context is DSP architecture 81. A V73 backend alone does
not make that compiled decoder portable. The complete graph I/O metadata was
recovered (three graphs, 1294 input/output entries and their quantization).
The shared weight blob is 1,878,839,296 opaque bytes; the available context
metadata does not expose its operator connectivity or individual base weights.
This investigation did not reconstruct the full decoder or establish that all
base weights can be recovered. It does not prove recovery is impossible.

The installed contextual_reply FlatBuffer contains 167 readable payloads.
164 LoRA matrices were matched by name, shape and byte count to the context
I/O; three forecast prefix/embedding payloads remain unmapped. Those adapters
are specific to the Samsung base model and cannot be attached to Qwen weights.

One recovered q_lora_d_1_A matrix [3072,32] was used in an inferred projection
on V73 through QNN graph construction, finalization and execution. All 32
outputs returned. Against an independent integer accumulator reference, the
maximum absolute error was 0.0012636780750119314, RMSE 0.0007427483400500312,
with no saturation. The predeclared tolerance was two output LSBs; observed
maximum was five LSBs. Therefore the strict numerical check FAILED. There was
no comparison against execution on the original Samsung donor hardware.

The diagnostic pins libQnnHtpPrepare.so until process exit to avoid a QNN
online-prepare teardown crash. This is not a production backend workaround.
The new replacement-model pipeline uses precompiled contexts instead.

Reproduction sources: inspect_adapter.py, probe_lora_matmul.c,
compare_projection.py, run_projection.sh in this directory.
Raw evidence: /tmp/gauss-v73-feasibility-20261014/

Decision: pursue the user-authorized replacement backend while retaining
Samsung routing; do not advertise reconstructed Samsung Gauss inference.
